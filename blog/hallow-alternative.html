Best Hallow Alternative (2026): BlessUp vs. Hallow

Hallow is a well-known prayer and meditation app. If you're searching for a Hallow alternative, you're probably after something a little simpler — or something that actually helps you build the habit. Here's how BlessUp compares.

The core difference

Hallow is a deep library of guided prayer and audio sessions. BlessUp takes a different angle: it locks your distracting apps until you pray. Instead of being one more app you have to remember to open, BlessUp builds prayer into the moment you reach for your phone.

Why people switch to BlessUp

  • Habit first. Prayer Lock turns "I'll pray later" into prayer right now.
  • Less doomscrolling. Your feed stays closed until you've spent a moment in faith.
  • Simple and free to start. No overwhelming menu — just pray and go.

Which is right for you?

If you want a vast catalog of guided audio, Hallow is great. If your real goal is to stop scrolling and actually start your day with prayer, BlessUp is the alternative built for that.

It's not just another app to open

The hardest part of any faith app is remembering to use it. Most apps sit on your phone hoping you'll tap them. BlessUp works the other way around: it meets you at the moment you'd normally start scrolling and turns it into prayer. That's why the habit sticks where others fade.

You can always use both, too — pray through BlessUp to unlock your day, then dive into a guided session elsewhere. But if you only download one, choose the one that actually changes your morning. BlessUp is free to start on the App Store.
