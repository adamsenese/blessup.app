The Best Bible Widget for Your iPhone Home Screen

Your home screen is the most-seen screen on your phone. Why not put Scripture right where your eyes already go? A Bible widget places a verse on your iPhone home screen so the Word meets you dozens of times a day.

A verse every time you unlock

With the BlessUp Bible Widget, a fresh verse sits among your apps. Every glance at your phone becomes a tiny reminder — no app to open, no step to remember. The Scripture is simply there.

How to add it

  • Touch and hold an empty area of your iPhone home screen.
  • Tap the + button in the top corner.
  • Search for BlessUp and choose the Bible Widget size you like.
  • Tap Add Widget, then place it where you'll see it most.

Small reminder, big difference

We become what we repeat. A verse you pass all day quietly shapes how you think — far more than one you'd only read if you remembered to open an app.

More than a pretty widget

A Bible widget isn't just decoration for your home screen. It's a steady, low-effort way to keep Scripture in front of you without adding one more task to your day. The verse is simply there, waiting, every time you check the time or open an app.

Pair the widget with a moment of prayer each morning and you've built a simple rhythm: see the Word, sit with it, then go. BlessUp brings both together — the widget on your home screen and prayer at the start of your day — and it's free to download.

Start your day with prayer, not doomscrolling. BlessUp is free on the App Store.
