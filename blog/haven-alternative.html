Looking for a Haven App Alternative? Try BlessUp

If you've been looking for a Haven app alternative, you want a tool that helps you slow down and put faith at the center of your day. BlessUp does exactly that — with one simple, powerful idea.

Prayer before your feed

BlessUp locks your most distracting apps until you pray. Rather than hoping you'll find a quiet moment later, it creates that moment the second you pick up your phone. Faith comes first, the feed comes after.

What makes BlessUp different

  • Prayer Lock keeps social media and news closed until you've prayed.
  • Bible Mode puts Scripture in front of you before you scroll.
  • A Bible Widget keeps a daily verse on your home screen.

Simple by design

You don't need another complicated app. BlessUp is built around a single habit that sticks: pray first, then go about your day.

Faith at the center of your day

It's easy to mean well and still let the day get away from you. By the time you remember to pause, you've already been scrolling for an hour. BlessUp closes that gap by making prayer the very first thing — not something you hope to fit in later.

If Haven helped you slow down and refocus, you'll feel right at home with BlessUp's quieter, faith-first approach. Give it a week and see how your mornings change. It's free to start on the App Store.
